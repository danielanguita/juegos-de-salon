## D-607 · Tango scores time in La Copa too, from the cups created with the new rule
**Date:** 2026-11-23 · **Status:** current · **Relation:** extends D-572 to La Copa · **Topics:** cup
**Context:** D-572 made Tango score the time on its own page and in Uno al día, and left La Copa on 10 a mistake so no running cup changed its rule mid-way, with cups to move over later. `tango-time` reached every player, and the owner found La Copa saying «Cada error resta 10» while the same game outside it says mistakes cost nothing: one game, two rules.
**Decision:** a cup created while `tango-time` is on for its creator plays Tango by time, like everywhere else. The creation writes `tango:time` in `meta.cal` (`NEW_RULES` in `public/cup/engine.js`, read like a variant but never offered to the admin), and the board carries `porTiempo`, so every player of that cup plays the same rule whatever their own flags; its rules text, mistakes line and breakdown read the time rule through `conVariante`. A cup created before keeps 10 a mistake to its end. The Grand Final carries the day's rule when it repeats Tango. Reinas needs nothing: it already scores the time in La Copa and outside it (D-107); only its short round of the final gives 100 for solving, with the final's time breaking ties, as every short round has its own rule (D-418).
**Why:** a player who learns a game in one place should not meet another rule in the other (the owner's request); writing the rule in the cup, not reading each player's flag, keeps a cup fair.
**Consequences:** `NEW_RULES` is the way for the next game that changes its rule: a flag, its entry, and the cups created from then on take it. When `tango-time` loses its gate, the cups created with it keep reading `tango:time`.
