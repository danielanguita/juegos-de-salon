## D-557 · Among the new games, the newest first
**Date:** 2026-11-14 · **Status:** current · **Relation:** amends D-335 (the new games go on top of the home page, by popularity among them) · **Topics:** home
**Context:** ⚡ ¡Al toque! launched on 2026-11-14 and took its ✨ Nuevo label (D-556), but on the home page it sat under Aletazo, launched the week before, because the new games were ordered by popularity among themselves. The owner asked for the newest first.
**Decision:** Right after La Copa, the games still new go from the newest to the oldest by the moment they became new (`nuevoEn` in games.js: the launch flag's moment, D-556, or `nuevoDesde`); the rest stay by popularity. `games.test.mjs` checks the order.
**Why:** the top of the list is where a returning player looks for what changed; the game launched tonight is the news.
**Consequences:** a new game that is played a lot no longer climbs over a newer one while both are new; once its label ends it goes back to its place by popularity.
