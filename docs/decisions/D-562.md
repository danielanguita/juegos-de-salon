## D-562 · La Copa: a reaction and a comment on each result, kept only while the cup lasts
**Date:** 2026-11-16 · **Status:** current · **Relation:** — · **Topics:** cup
**Context:** A player asked for a chat to talk with the rest of their cup (feedback [#1488](https://github.com/ellokojavi/juegos-de-salon/issues/1488)). The room chat (C-15, D-28) lives as long as a live room; a cup lasts days and its players play at different times, so a chat there would have to be kept. The owner chose (2026-11-16) a prototype in the lab: each day, a reaction and a comment on each player's result, from the active cup's board.
**Decision:**
- **Behind the flag `cup-talk`** (card #seccion-cup-talk in /labs/). Each row of the cup's table has a 💬 under the points, with how many reactions and comments that player got; tapping it opens a drawer under the row with their result of each day you both played, one day at a time (newest first). (First a separate panel under the table; the owner moved it into the table the same day, 2026-11-16.)
- **Per author, per day, per result: one reaction and one comment.** Six fixed reactions (🔥 😂 👏 😮 😢 👑), changeable or taken back; a comment of up to 120 characters, one line, that cannot be edited, only deleted. Your own result too (the owner: yes).
- **Only after playing that day** (the owner: yes), so nobody comments before knowing how their own day went, and only while the cup is on.
- **Moderation is the target's and the filter's:** the player who got a comment can hide it from everyone (they still see it, marked) and show it again; the author can delete theirs; the cup's admin can delete anything with the cup. The name filter (D-513) stops offensive words before sending; the rules do not repeat it.
- **Stored apart and deleted when the cup ends:** `cupTalk/<code>/<day>/<target>/<author>` `{ e, c, at }` and `cupTalkHidden/<code>/<day>/<target>/<author>` (apart so the author cannot undo a hide). Public to read like the cup. The nightly `archive.mjs` deletes both, not archives them, once the cup ended, was ended early or deleted; deleting a cup deletes them in the same write. The privacy page says so in three languages.
**Why:** Reactions and short comments on a result give the cup's players something to say to each other without the cost of a free chat that lasts days: no thread to moderate, nothing to read before playing, and nothing kept after the cup. Hiding is the target's because the comment is on their result.
**Consequences:** Not offline (the panel is not drawn from the offline copy). While a comment is being written, the board is not redrawn under the keyboard; it is when the field lets go. If the owner launches it, the gate is removed in a later PR and the card goes to La Bodega (D-510).
