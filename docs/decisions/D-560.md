## D-560 · Counts of games leave lab plays out, in the panel and the weekly report
**Date:** 2026-11-15 · **Status:** current · **Relation:** amends D-348 (the panel by domain) and D-352 (the weekly report) · **Topics:** panel, publishing
**Context:** The weekly report of Nov 8–14 counted 75 lab plays (¡Al toque! before its launch, Sudoku before Nov 12, artifacts, transfer, memory) in Individual games, Games started and Games finished: about 10% of them. The panel computes the same numbers the same way.
**Decision:** Every count of games reads the days through `withoutLabGames(days, publicGameCheck(flags))` (`public/panel/public-games.js`): a play counts only if its game is in `ALL_GAMES` and is neither a lab game (`labs`) nor in La Bodega, or is one of La Copa's games without a page; lab cups (`meta.lab`) are left out too, and, for a game a flag launched, from the UTC day its flag went to *all*. The panel's live views (rooms in progress, logs, a game's own page) keep showing lab plays. The weekly report also reads a game's versions as one (Bulls and Cows with Word and Number; Paint Mines' difficulties).
**Why:** The report and the panel speak about players; lab plays are the owner's and the testers' (D-395). One function keeps the two from disagreeing.
**Consequences:** Counts dropped where lab games were played (Nov 8–14: games finished 620 → 574). A game's numbers start on its launch day. A new lab game needs nothing: outside `ALL_GAMES` it is out of the counts on its own.
