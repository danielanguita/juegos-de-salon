## D-592 · The «T» tab has two views: what is being tested, and every experiment
**Date:** 2026-11-20 · **Status:** current · **Relation:** amends D-567 · **Topics:** structure
**Context:** With every lab flag of a page listed with its switch (D-567), plus the offline-mode switch and the screen's own control, the tab opened as a long, dense panel. The owner (2026-11-19): a second view where the tester picks what they are testing, so a feature's switch can be hidden without removing it (the offline-mode one, for example). The owner chose to ship it straight away, without a lab flag: "Si nos equivocamos, revertimos".
**Decision:**
- **First view, «Probando ahora»:** only the rows the tester pinned with 📌, the way to the second view («Experimentos · N»), Labs and the screen's control (`setTesterContext`), which always stays there.
- **Second view, «Experimentos»:** every row the page has (each lab flag, and the offline-mode switch), each with its 📌 and its switch, in a wider panel.
- **Hiding is not turning off:** a pin only shows or hides a row, on this phone (`juegos-de-salon:flags-pinned`). A row out of its normal state (a flag turned off, offline mode forced) is never hidden: it shows in the first view, marked, pinned or not.
- **A screen's control can go with a row** (`setTesterContext(node, { bundle })`, the owner, 2026-11-19): the cloud states («Estados de la nube») go with the offline-mode row, shown in the first view only while that row is (pinned or forced on), hidden with it, and never in the second view.
- **New experiments come in unpinned**, so the tab does not fill up again. Folded, the tab opens again on the first view.
**Why:** The tester sees only what they are trying, and nothing that is changed on their phone can be forgotten behind the second view.
**Consequences:** `labPinnedList`/`setLabPinned` in `flags.js`, the views in `tester-ribbon.js`; `flags.test.mjs` and `tools/e2e/home/tester-tab.mjs` check them. D-567 still holds: every lab flag gets its switch by itself, now in the second view.
