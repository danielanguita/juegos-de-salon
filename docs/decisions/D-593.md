## D-593 · A card sent to La Bodega as 💤 loses its switch in the «T» tab
**Date:** 2026-11-20 · **Status:** current · **Relation:** extends D-567 and D-511 · **Topics:** structure
**Context:** La Copa's home card context (`copa-contexto`) went to La Bodega as 💤 No prosperó, but its flag was never switched off, so it stayed *lab* and the «T» tab kept its switch. The owner made it a rule (2026-11-19): "Como regla general, todas las tarjetas que pasan a la bodega deben ser removidas de la tarjeta T. Esto es distinto a un feature lanzado."
**Decision:** a flag whose card is in La Bodega as 💤 No prosperó is in `PARKED` (`public/assets/js/flag-list.js`), and the «T» tab shows no switch for it, whatever its state. A launched flag (*all*) already has none (D-567); this is the other way out of the lab. The 📦 Enviar a La Bodega job adds the id when it moves a 💤 card with a `FLAGS` row (`tools/jobs/prompts/lab-publish.md`). A flag removed from its pages altogether (like `copa-contexto`) leaves `FLAGS` and needs no entry.
**Why:** the T tab is for what is still being tried; what did not prosper is not tried any more, and its switch only gets in the way.
**Consequences:** 🤖 `public/labs/listed.test.mjs` requires `PARKED` to equal La Bodega's 💤 cards that have a `FLAGS` row, both ways. `lab-ready`'s own exception in `tester-ribbon.js` became this rule.
