## D-558 · A weekly cost check, and no saving that costs quality
**Date:** 2026-11-15 · **Status:** current · **Relation:** — · **Topics:** agents, tests
**Context:** The owner asked for less time testing and fewer tokens, then set the condition: no change may degrade quality or performance. One pass (PRs #1470 and this one) ran the unit tests in parallel (2 min → under 1 on GitHub), split the three slowest files across worker threads with the same checks, and moved the processes agent's feedback step to `docs/FEEDBACK-TRIAGE.md`, read only when there is feedback. The owner asked the processes agent to keep looking as the app grows.
**Decision:** Once a week the processes round runs `node tools/agents/costs.mjs` (slowest tests, the text every session and agent loads, docs too big to read whole, PR-check minutes) and takes on at most one saving as the round's mechanism. A saving may make the same work cheaper (run in parallel, avoid repeated setup, read on demand what is not always needed); it may never drop a test case, a check, a rule or speed for players. The proof goes in the PR: the same tests and counts pass, a moved text is identical.
**Why:** the app and its docs grow every week, so the costs grow by themselves; a fixed check finds them while they are small, and the condition keeps a saving from being paid for in quality.
**Consequences:** `costs.mjs` (tests, always-loaded text, big docs, PR checks, end to end, Actions minutes, Claude tokens from this machine's transcripts) and `run-tests.mjs`'s `TIMES=n`; the round's step 6 names the check; `docs/COSTS.md` explains the numbers and the levers.
