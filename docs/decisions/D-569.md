## D-569 · The Mac mini's chores without Claude ride the heartbeat: feedback upload and lab readiness
**Date:** 2026-11-17 · **Status:** current · **Relation:** amends D-382, D-528 · **Topics:** agents
**Context:** The owner wants nothing that matters to live only on the Mac mini: if it dies, the work must go on from the repository. `feedback-watch` (every 5 minutes) and `lab-ready` (hourly) moved to the Mac in D-382 because GitHub's schedules ran late or not at all; since D-564 the heartbeat (`notifications.yml`) is a relay that does run every 5 minutes.
**Decision:** Both run inside the heartbeat's loop: `feedback.mjs sync` every round, `lab-ready.mjs check` each hour next to Uno al día's rebuild, with the same FIREBASE_ADMIN_JSON secret and the workflow token (issues: write). In `jobs.json` they are `github` jobs on `notifications.yml` with a trigger, not a cron of their own; their launchd jobs are uninstalled from the Mac.
**Why:** No Claude and no cost: the repository is public and the minutes are free. The heartbeat is already measured and watched (D-565), so a stop is noticed.
**Consequences:** A failure of either only warns and the next round retries; it never stops the notifications. `feedback.yml` stays as the manual fallback.
