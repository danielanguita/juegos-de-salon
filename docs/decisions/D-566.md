## D-566 · La Copa: a balloon tells players they can comment, once per phone
**Date:** 2026-11-16 · **Status:** current · **Relation:** completes D-562 · **Topics:** cup
**Context:** Comments on the cup's table (D-562) were launched for everyone, but on the first day only the three comments from the lab existed: nothing tells a player the 💬 is new. The owner asked (2026-11-16) for a dismissable balloon, in the lab first; a site-wide splash was set aside because most visitors are not in a cup.
**Decision:** Behind the flag `cup-talk-hint` (card #seccion-cup-talk-hint in /labs/): a yellow balloon at the top of the cup's table, «💬 ¡Ahora puedes comentar la ronda del día! Deja tu comentario en la fila de cualquier jugador.» (a call to action, the owner 2026-11-16), shown only when the 💬 are (the player already played today). ✕ closes it, and so does using any 💬; either way it never comes back on that phone, in any cup (`cuenta.pistaCharla`, localStorage). It says "comment", not "chat", so nobody expects a full chat.
**Why:** The one who can comment learns it where the 💬 is, at the moment it works; the one who gets a comment learns it from the notification (D-564).
**Consequences:** Once launched and old enough that every regular player saw it, a later PR removes it with its flag.
