## D-604 · A player's activity shows on their public profile unless they hide it
**Date:** 2026-11-22 · **Status:** current · **Relation:** amends D-602 · **Topics:** rankings
**Context:** D-602 kept a player's activity (streak, games, since when, most played games and the achievements that count how much they play) off their public profile until they turned it on. The owner saw that, off by default, nobody would ever show it.
**Decision:** The activity shows by default. A player hides it with «Mostrar mi actividad» in ⚙️ 👁️ Tu perfil público, stored as `jugadores/<jid>/oculto: true` and removed when shown again. The rule moves from `abierto` to `oculto`: same writers, only `true` or gone. While it shows, a yellow bubble under the switch says «👀 Tus juegos son visibles para otros jugadores». It shows until its ✕, or until the player hides the activity (`juegos-de-salon:profile-public-bubble`). «Ver cómo me ven los demás» and a tap on your own name open exactly the sheet others see, with no words of their own.
**Why:** a choice nobody makes is a default. Showing it makes the profile worth a tap, and the bubble at the switch tells each player once, where they can change it. A sheet with its own words for yourself would not show what others really see.
**Consequences:** Players who turned it on under D-602 stored `abierto: true`. It is no longer read, and they show as everyone does now. The privacy page says the activity shows and can be hidden. `firebase/public-profile.test.mjs` checks the new rule in the rules and in the test store.
