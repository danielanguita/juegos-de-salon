## D-584 · The live site's /labs/ pages write to their own stats environment, `labs`
**Date:** 2026-11-18 · **Status:** current · **Relation:** amends D-560 (lab plays out of the counts) and D-471 (the `owner` environment) · **Topics:** panel, publishing
**Context:** D-560 took lab plays out of the counts of games, but visits, page views, users, traffic, health and the timeline still counted what happened on `/labs/` pages: the owner and the testers trying things. The owner asked that the panel's data leave Labs activity out.
**Decision:** On the live site, a page under `/labs/` writes its signals to `stats/labs/` instead of `stats/prod/` (`envFor` and `isLabsPath` in `transport/stats.js`, the same check in `vigia.js`), even from a not-counted browser. The panel lists it as **Labs** in its environment picker and reads `prod` by default, so every number leaves it out. Tests and previews stay `dev`. Rankings, rooms and La Copa are untouched: only the stats environment changes. A tester playing a public game still counts: only `/labs/` pages are Labs activity.
**Why:** A separate environment, as for `owner` (D-471), rather than dropping the signals: the owner can still see how the lab is used, and no number has to filter page by page.
**Consequences:** From the release on, `/labs/` visits and plays stop reaching Live. Data saved before stays in `prod`; its lab plays were already out of the counts of games (D-560). `stats/<env>` already accepted any short lowercase name, so the Firebase rules did not change.
