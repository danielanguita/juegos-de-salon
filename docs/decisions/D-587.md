## D-587 · The Grand Final ranks each round by place, like a day
**Date:** 2026-11-18 · **Status:** current · **Relation:** amends D-113 for the Grand Final's ranking; D-417's running score stays · **Topics:** cup
**Context:** A player of Thursday's Guys #7 (KYVBL, issue #1615) asked that the final score like the days:
whoever plays a round fastest gets 10 points, instead of 100 for everyone who completes it. The final was
the average of its rounds (0 to 100 each), so a game scored by time (☀️ Tango, 〰️ Zip, 👑 Reinas) gave
100 to everyone who solved it, and its time only counted inside the final's whole time.
**Decision:** In a final that opens at `FINAL_PLACES_FROM` or later (`finalByPlaces(meta)` in
`public/cup/engine.js`), each round hands out the day's points by place (`PUNTOS`: its score, then that
round's own time), the final is ranked by their sum (then by its whole time), and the final's place gives
the usual points, doubled. Each result keeps its rounds as `rs` (`"score/ms"` per round, allowed by the
Firebase rules); `posicionesDelDia` ranks by rounds only when every result of the day carries them, so a
final already played, or one with a result sent by an old phone, keeps the average for everyone. The day
table of the final shows each player's points in rounds and their place in each one; the rules say
it in three languages (`puntajeLugares`). Between rounds the player still sees their own average (D-417):
the places are only known when the others play.
**Why:** The days rank by place and the time breaks ties; the final should not flatten that. The cut by
date and by data keeps every final already open (KYVBL's included) on the rule it started with.
**Consequences:** The weekend cup's day of three (D-439) and the final outside a cup keep the average.
