## D-556 · A game launched by a flag is new from its launch
**Date:** 2026-11-14 · **Status:** current · **Relation:** amends D-333 (the "New" label counts from `nuevoDesde`); builds on the launch flags of D-517 and D-529 · **Topics:** home, structure
**Context:** ⚡ ¡Al toque! launched at 21:00 Pacific on 2026-11-14 with `nuevoDesde: '2026-11-15'`, written ahead as "the day after", so its home card went out without the ✨ Nuevo label. The owner noticed and asked to fix it and make it a rule.
**Decision:** A game with `lanza` is new from the moment its flag went to *all* (the `at` that `flags/<id>` keeps, written by the /labs/ switch or the `launch` workflow), or from `nuevoDesde` if that is earlier; the label lasts its `diasNuevo` (or DIAS_NUEVO) from there. `esNuevo` in games.js reads it from the flags the phone last saw; `games.test.mjs` checks a launch the evening before its written date. A flag only for the lab does not make it new.
**Why:** the launch is the moment the players first see the game; a date written ahead of time can miss it by hours, and the label is most useful on that first night.
**Consequences:** a flag-launched game no longer needs its `nuevoDesde` guessed right; it still carries one (D-333's test). ¡Al toque! gets `nuevoDesde: '2026-11-14'` and 10 days, as Sudoku and Aletazo.
