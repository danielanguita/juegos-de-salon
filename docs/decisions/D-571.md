## D-571 · «Instala Juegos de Salón»: the invitation snoozes instead of leaving for good, and is always in the footer
**Date:** 2026-11-17 · **Status:** current · **Relation:** amends D-232 · **Topics:** pwa
**Context:** Players keep asking why Juegos de Salón is not in the App Store. The home page bubble (D-232) said «Agrégala a tu pantalla de inicio», words people do not search for, and its ✕ turned it off forever: whoever closed it once had no way back. The home page is already crowded, so a new permanent element there costs too much. The owner asked (2026-11-17) for an easier invitation, worded as "install", with an interesting visual treatment, in the lab.
**Decision:** Behind the flag `install-invite` (card #seccion-install-invite in /labs/): the invitation says «Instala Juegos de Salón · Gratis y sin App Store», in an iridescent card (a border that turns through the brand's colours, a sheen). On the home page it waits until the phone finished a first game. Its ✕ snoozes it 14 days, then 60 each time; only «Ya la instalé», Chrome's install or the installed app end it. A moment after any game ends (`trackFinish`, every game, C-21) it invites from the top, once a week at most, and leaves by itself. On iPhone the steps draw Safari's buttons (⋯ from iOS 26, Share before) and an arrow points at Safari's toolbar. The home footer keeps «📲 Instalar la app» among its tiles, so it is always at hand without a new element above the fold. Beside the title, «sin instalar» becomes «sin descargas», which stays true.
**Why:** "Install" is the word people bring from the stores; the moment after a game is when the site has earned it; a snooze respects a "not now" without losing the player for good; the footer gives a way back at no cost to the home page.
**Consequences:** When launched, a later PR removes the old bubble's texts and the flag, and «sin instalar» changes to «sin descargas» in every page's description too (they are static HTML, not behind the flag).
