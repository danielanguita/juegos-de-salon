## D-613 · A game has the same rules in La Copa and outside it
**Date:** 2026-11-23 · **Status:** current · **Relation:** extends C-21; follows D-607 · **Topics:** cup
**Context:** D-572 and D-607 moved Tango to scoring the time step by step, and the cups already running kept 10 a mistake to their end. The owner had asked that neither Tango nor Reinas punish a mistake, in La Copa or outside it, and set the general rule: the games are the same in and out of La Copa.
**Decision:** a game plays by one rule (how it scores, what a mistake or a hint costs, how it ends) on its own page, in Uno al día and in La Copa. La Copa only adds what a cup needs: the day it is played, its variants chosen by the admin, the Grand Final's short rounds (D-418), the wildcard, the table. A rule change reaches La Copa in the same PR as outside it; a running cup moves over at the next day of that game nobody has played yet, never in the middle of a day, by rewriting its `meta.cal`. Done for Tango (D-607): Copa Galvez 3 (GTJWP) at once, Thursday Guys #8 (NZLTT) once its Tango day closed, so its Grand Final too.
**Why:** a player who learns a game in one place should not meet another rule in the other (the owner's tenet); a day played with two rules would be unfair, so the switch waits for the day.
**Consequences:** C-21 says it. A game that cannot take a rule in La Copa writes why in its spec (C-13).
