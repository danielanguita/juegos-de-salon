## D-598 · Críptico: three clues a day, scored out of 100
**Date:** 2026-11-20 · **Status:** current · **Relation:** — · **Topics:** random-and-daily
**Context:** Críptico gave one clue a day worth 3 stars, a star off per hint. The owner asked for a day of
three clues, easy, medium and hard, that the player must get through to finish, a score of 100 that hints
bring down, a hint costing more on a harder clue, and a shared pool of hints the player can save for the
hard one. Straight into the game, with no flag (it is a lab game).
**Decision:** Each clue in `clues.js` has a level (`l`: e, m or h). A day plays one of each in that order,
each level walked on its own (`dayPicks`). They are worth 20, 30 and 50. The day has 3 hints for its three
clues (still up to the three kinds on one clue); a hint costs 5 on the easy clue, 8 on the medium one and 15
on the hard one. Wrong guesses are free; giving up scores 0 for that clue and moves on. The streak counts the
days finished, whatever the score. Practice is one clue of any level, with its own 3 hints, scored out of its
worth and counting for nothing.
**Why:** 20/30/50 makes the hard clue worth more than the other two together, so it is where the day is won.
A hint costs about a quarter of the easy and medium clues and nearly a third of the hard one, so it costs
more where it helps most; even with three hints a solved clue scores more than giving up (5, 6 or 5), so
trying always pays. The score already measures skill: the streak only asks the player to come back.
**Consequences:** Clues repeat sooner (17 easy, 21 medium, 20 hard: the easy ones come back after 17 days),
so the bank needs clues of every level, the easy ones first. A day saved on a phone before this change (one
clue) is left behind: that day can be played again as three clues.
