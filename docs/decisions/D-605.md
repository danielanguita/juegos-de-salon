## D-605 · A public profile has nothing to hide: places, not play counts
**Date:** 2026-11-23 · **Status:** current · **Relation:** replaces D-602, D-604 · **Topics:** rankings
**Context:** D-602 hid a player's activity on their public profile until they showed it, and D-604 turned that round: shown unless they hid it with «Mostrar mi actividad». Then the sheet's most played games stopped saying how many times each was played and said the player's all-time place in it instead (PR #1781). The owner saw that, with that, almost everything on the sheet is already public in the rankings, and what is left (the streak, the total of games and the achievements that count them) is not serious.
**Decision:** No switch. Every player's sheet shows the same thing to everyone: their flag and name, since when they play, their overall place in the top 10, their streak, games and cups won, their three most played games with their all-time place in each, and their public achievements. «Ver cómo me ven los demás» stays in ⚙️ 👁️ Tu perfil público; the switch, its yellow bubble and the hidden sheet go. The Firebase rule for `jugadores/<jid>/oculto` goes too, and the field is refused like `abierto`.
**Why:** a place says how well someone plays, not how much, and the rankings already show it. A switch that protects almost nothing is one more thing to explain, and a sheet that differs from player to player is less worth a tap.
**Consequences:** The few lab testers who hid their activity under D-604 keep a stray `oculto: true`; it is no longer read. The privacy page says what the sheet shows, with nothing to hide. `firebase/public-profile.test.mjs` checks that both old fields are refused.
