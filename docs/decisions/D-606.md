## D-606 · A PR that launches a game cannot merge until launch-check passes on its screens
**Date:** 2026-11-23 · **Status:** current · **Relation:** — · **Topics:** tests, structure
**Context:** Croc! and Tembleque launched on 2026-11-22 with places missing (no ranking on Tembleque's cover, their /labs/ cards not moving to La Bodega) although `docs/LAUNCH.md` listed them: the session did not walk the playbook. `tools/agents/launch-check.mjs` (#1797) checks every place a launched game shows, but running it was left to the session's memory.
**Decision:** The `end-to-end` check (`.github/workflows/e2e.yml`, required on main, D-216) has a `launch` job: `tools/agents/launching.mjs` names the games a PR launches (a game already behind its flag, `lanza`, at the base whose launch day, `nuevoDesde`, moves: the launch-day PR, LAUNCH.md §3), and `launch-check.mjs <ids> --screens` must pass on them. A ✗ turns `end-to-end` red, so the PR cannot merge. The preparation PR (the game appears with its `lanza`) and the cleanup PR (`lanza` goes) are not launches.
**Why:** a check nobody is forced to run is a list to read again; the owner asked that the next launch be whole without depending on anyone remembering the playbook.
**Consequences:** The launch-day PR needs everything `launch-check` asks for already in `main` or in the PR: the splash campaign, the link card, the spec, the e2e folder, the cover's ranking. What it cannot see (the splash on a real phone, La Copa's create screen as a new visitor, `/labs/` with a lab key) is still looked at by eye after the hour (LAUNCH.md §3). A miss found later adds its check to `launch-check.mjs`, and so to this gate.
