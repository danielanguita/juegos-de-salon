## D-561 · The owner's timeframes are Pacific time: the WBR, the weekly email and every period the owner reads
**Date:** 2026-11-16 · **Status:** current · **Relation:** amends D-352 (its week was UTC days) and D-207 (the panel's days are UTC) · **Topics:** panel
**Context:** The WBR and the weekly email cut their weeks, months and days at midnight UTC, which is 16:00 or 17:00 in Pacific time, the owner's clock: an evening's games landed on the next day, and a week's Saturday night on the next week. The owner asked for Pacific time in the WBR and in every timeframe calculation, and for the rule to be kept.
**Decision:** A day, week or month the owner reads is a Pacific date (`America/Los_Angeles`, PST/PDT), never a UTC day. The stats keep their UTC-day buckets (`stats/<env>/days/<utc day>`); `toPacificDays()` (`public/panel/wbr.js`) regroups them by the Pacific date of each record's moment: offline games from `live/` (their `local/` and `fin/` rebuilt from it, on the days `live/` matches them, from 2026-10-04), rooms by `at`, cups by their results' moments (`resumenCopas`/`embudoCopas` take `dayFn`). Active browsers and visits carry no moment, so from this decision on the site also writes them by Pacific day (`activosPt/<pacific day>/<id>`, `entradasPt/<pacific day>/<page>`, same values and rules as `activos/` and `entradas/`). The WBR, the weekly email (every section) and the closed week (`closedWeek`) read Pacific dates. Any new period, range or "today" built for the owner is Pacific from the start.
**Why:** The owner reads the numbers on Pacific time; a day that ends at 16:00 is not their day, and a week that does is not the week they lived.
**Consequences:** Games are by Pacific date from 2026-10-04 on; before that (no `live/`) they stay on UTC days. Browsers and visits are by Pacific date from 2026-11-16 on; earlier weeks keep UTC days, said on the WBR and in the PDF's footer. Every panel view (Summary, Group, Individual, Users, Traffic, Health, Campaigns, Timeline) counts Pacific dates through `toPacificDays`; counters with no time (failures, shares, campaign reach) keep their UTC day. No new data about players: the same pseudonymous id and counters, kept as long as their day (stats-retention).
