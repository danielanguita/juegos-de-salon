## D-602 · A player's activity stays private on their public profile unless they show it
**Date:** 2026-11-22 · **Status:** current · **Relation:** — · **Topics:** rankings
**Context:** A tap on a name in a ranking opens that player's profile at a glance (lab flag `profile`, `public/profile/peek.js`). It showed their streak, their game count, since when they play and how many times they played each game. The owner asked whether someone who plays a lot might not want others to know, and chose that each player decides.
**Decision:** The sheet always shows the flag and name, the place in the overall ranking when in its top 10, and the achievements public data can tell, except those that count how much someone plays (`ACTIVITY_BADGES`: streaks, games played, days, a full month, a year). The activity (streak, games, cups won, since when, the three most played games with their counts, and those achievements) shows only if the player turns on «Mostrar mi actividad» in ⚙️ 👁️ Tu perfil público. That is stored as `jugadores/<jid>/abierto: true` (absent when off), written only from one of the player's seated phones. Off is the default. «Ver cómo me ven los demás» in the same place, and a tap on your own name, show your own sheet as others see it. The privacy page says it.
**Why:** a player who never opens ⚙️ is protected without doing anything, and those who don't mind can show more. The data a player wants to show off (achievements, places) is visible either way.
**Consequences:** This hides the activity on screen only. The counts in `jugadores/<jid>/juegos` and the history in `unoAlDia/<jid>` stay readable by anyone under the rules, as before this sheet existed. Closing them would mean moving what the rankings and Uno al día read, which is a separate change. The test store (`jugador-local.js`) mirrors the new rule, and `firebase/public-profile.test.mjs` checks both.
