## D-575 · The owner approves a 🔴 PR once: "Update branch" keeps the tick, the repo's workflows are insiders, and a session ticks with one command
**Date:** 2026-11-17 · **Status:** current · **Relation:** amends D-519, D-540 · **Topics:** agents
**Context:** The owner asks sessions for work, then had to tick the same PR again and again in the PR page: #1503 (weekly popularity) took three taps in one afternoon, one for each "Update branch", and was 🔴 at all only because a bot opened it. Sessions that may tick for the owner (D-540) often forgot to.
**Decision:** (1) A push that only merges main into an approved commit (two parents, the other one in main's history, and its tree is exactly what a clean merge gives, `git merge-tree`) carries the approval: `lanes.mjs ci` sets `owner-approval` green and turns on auto-merge. Any other push asks again. (2) `github-actions[bot]` is an insider: only this repository's workflows push as it, a fork cannot, so its PRs take the lane of their files. (3) `node tools/agents/lanes.mjs tick <n>` ticks the Yes in the PR's description; `lanes.mjs check` reminds the session of it on 🔴, and the stalled-PR watch (D-573) covers the bot's PRs too.
**Why:** The owner's Yes is about the PR's own change; merging main in does not change it, and the check refuses any merge that brings anything else. The bot's PRs were never from outside.
**Consequences:** A 🔴 PR asks the owner once per real change. The owner taps only PRs that sessions did not open at their request.
