## D-572 · Tango scores time, not mistakes, as Reinas does (outside La Copa first)
**Date:** 2026-11-17 · **Status:** current · **Relation:** extends D-107 to Tango · **Topics:** cup
**Context:** Reinas scores the time and mistakes cost nothing (D-107); Tango took 10 a mistake and 15 a hint from 100, and the time only broke ties. Two logic puzzles with two rules were hard to keep in mind, and Tango scored a 40-second solve and an 8-minute one alike.
**Decision:** Tango scores the time like Reinas: 100 up to 45 s, then evenly down to 10 at 5 min; mistakes cost nothing; each hint adds 15 s to the time (it must still cost something). It goes behind the lab flag `tango-time` and only outside La Copa (its own page and Uno al día, `ctx.suelto`), so no running cup changes its rule mid-way; cups move over in a later PR, for cups created after it. Old and new scores live side by side in the rankings, and the new ones replace the old as people play (the owner's call).
**Why:** punishing trial and error in a deduction game rewards caution, not skill (D-107); the 1.5 s red wait (D-337) already makes guessing cost time.
**Consequences:** the 45 s / 5 min curve is a first guess, to recalibrate from real solve times while in the lab.
