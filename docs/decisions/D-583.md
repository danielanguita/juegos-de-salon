## D-583 · Agentes' boards follow M3, and its decks lose their give-aways
**Date:** 2026-11-17 · **Status:** current · **Relation:** follows the owner's rubric of learning piece 1 (D-532) · **Topics:** agents-game
**Context:** The clue checks (`tools/generators/clues/`) found 18 give-aways in the three decks (a `si` word that contains its clue: MIDNIGHT for NIGHT, PESCADOR for PESCA) and that 17–19% of boards had a clue with as many of its temptations as agents, where the clue's number cannot pick the group (M3). The owner's plan was a lab flag, `agents-m3`, measured on the panel; but every Agentes board is seeded and shared (Cups, the standalone game, rankings), so a flag only lab key holders see would score them on other boards than everyone else.
**Decision:** Both go to everyone at once, in one PR on a day no running Cup has Agentes left: the 18 words are replaced in es/en/pt, and `generar` rejects a board that breaks M3 (it tries again, as it already does for other rules). No lab flag. `clues.test.mjs` keeps the decks at zero M1/M2 hits and the boards at zero M3.
**Why:** A rule about how boards are dealt cannot be tried by some players only without breaking fair scores; one reshuffle is better than two. The owner chose to do it (2026-11-17) and left the flag's shape to the session.
**Consequences:** Every Agentes board changes from this release, so before/after is a clean cut: the panel's numbers for Agentes (mean score, assassin touches, abandons) from the release date tell whether deducing got easier; the content piece's status records them after two weeks. Boards already played keep their stored results; only new deals change.
