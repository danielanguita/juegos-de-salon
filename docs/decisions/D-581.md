## D-581 · One a Day ends on one page
**Date:** 2026-11-17 · **Status:** current · **Relation:** builds on C-19, D-353 · **Topics:** random-and-daily
**Context:** Some games in One a Day show a screen between the last move and the result (a summary, a score, a step to tap through) before the page that carries One a Day's card. It costs a tap and splits what the player wants to see in one glance.
**Decision:** In today mode the last move goes straight to one result page. What an in-between screen showed becomes a block of that page in C-19's order (hero → One a Day card → detail → rankings → feedback). Games that have one migrate when a PR touches their result; new games are born this way (docs/ADD-GAME.md).
**Why:** The owner (2026-11-17): remove the unneeded interstitial pages, or move their widgets to a single final page.
**Consequences:** Judged, not checked: the usability agent and the contributions agent look for it; a game's in-between screen is debt to pay when its result is touched.
