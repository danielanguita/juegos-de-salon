## D-579 · A game enters the lab with its canons
**Date:** 2026-11-17 · **Status:** current · **Relation:** builds on D-395, D-542 · **Topics:** structure, tests
**Context:** Lab games came in without the cover, result-screen and game canons and got them one by one later: Blackjack's result screen lacked the rankings, the mode switch and the feedback box until the owner asked (#1551). A contributor could not know what a lab game owes before it merges.
**Decision:** Every lab game passes its canons before its first PR merges, ours or a contributor's: a `game.json` (so the conformance suite runs over it), its cover in `tools/e2e/paths.mjs` (measured by `landing-fold.mjs`), its result exits from `salidasFin` and its result in the rankings (C-19). `public/labs/canons.test.mjs` checks them in the `tests` check; the lab games older than the rule owe what `docs/labs-canon-debt.json` lists, a ratchet that only shrinks. A contributor's PR comes with them built; the contributions agent only checks.
**Why:** The owner (2026-11-17): every game that enters the lab must have the cover, result and game canons to pass, for us and for contributors, who bring them already built.
**Consequences:** A new lab game takes a little longer to open; the lab's games look and close like the published ones from day one, and launching one stops being a rebuild. CONTRIBUTING.md and the PR template carry the rule (generated from `docs/rules.json`).
