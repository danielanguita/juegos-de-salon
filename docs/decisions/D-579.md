## D-579 · A game enters the lab with its canons
**Date:** 2026-11-17 · **Status:** current · **Relation:** builds on D-395, D-542; amends D-304 (the lab's short review is of usability, not of canons) · **Topics:** structure, tests
**Context:** Lab games came in without the cover, result-screen and game canons and got them one by one later: Blackjack's result screen lacked the rankings, the mode switch and the feedback box until the owner asked (#1551). A contributor could not know what a lab game owes before it merges, and the generator itself made a game without the cover dialogs, the rankings, a measured cover or a languages script.
**Decision:** Every lab game passes the whole checklist of CANONS.md before its first PR merges, ours or a contributor's. `public/labs/canons.test.mjs` (in the `tests` check) fails one without its `game.json`, its cover in `tools/e2e/paths.mjs` (measured by `landing-fold.mjs`), its result exits from `salidasFin`, its result in the rankings, `coverWithDialogs`, `trackStart`/`trackFinish`, its spec, or its e2e play and languages scripts. Every other checklist line names its test or its 👁 reviewer in ADD-GAME.md, "The entry gate". The generator's game passes all of it as born. The lab games older than the rule owe what `docs/labs-canon-debt.json` lists, a ratchet that only shrinks and that a game pays before it leaves the lab. A contributor's PR comes with the canons built; the contributions agent only checks.
**Why:** The owner (2026-11-17): every game that enters the lab must have the cover, result and game canons, for us and for contributors, who bring them already built, and none of the good practices may be left out.
**Consequences:** A new lab game takes a little longer to open; the lab's games look and close like the published ones from day one, and launching one stops being a rebuild. CONTRIBUTING.md, the PR template (generated from `docs/rules.json`), CANONS.md's checklist and the contributions agent carry the rule. `tools/e2e/paths.mjs` gains `routeOf`, so a lab game not yet in `games.js` is measured at its own folder.
