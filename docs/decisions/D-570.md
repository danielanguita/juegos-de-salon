## D-570 · Every visual change is measured before it ships, and nothing may cover the next block
**Date:** 2026-11-17 · **Status:** current · **Relation:** completes D-533 · **Topics:** identity, agents
**Context:** Reviewing Blackjack's new cover (PR #1522), the owner had to ask for the margins to be checked, and the check found the demo covering the title by 9 px and 44 px of air where the other games have 32. No e2e check saw it: M-1…M-3 measure columns, card padding and media in a card, not one block sitting on the next. The owner (2026-11-17): "Quiero que la regla de verificar márgenes sea una constante cada vez que hagamos tratamiento visual de elementos, ya sea como un nuevo desarrollo o como una mejora a algo existente. Muchas veces tengo que pedírtelo."
**Decision:**
- **M-4, a check:** in a column, no block in the flow (a picture, a demo, a panel, a title, a text) starts more than 4 px above the bottom of the block before it. Measured on every `b.shot()` with M-1…M-3 (`tools/e2e/margins-common.mjs`) and reported by `look.mjs`; a ✗ fails `end-to-end`. Rows and what floats on purpose (absolute, fixed, sticky) are left out; a deliberate exception is marked `data-margins-ok` with its reason.
- **DESIGN.md rule 10, the process:** every change to how something looks, new or a retouch, is shot by its e2e script in each of its states, measured by the session with `look.mjs` at 390 and 320 px before the PR, and the PR says what was measured. It is in the canons' checklist and in CLAUDE.md's rules every session follows.
**Why:** What the owner's eye catches on a phone has to be caught first by a program where it can be measured, and by the session's own measuring where it cannot, so the owner never has to ask.
**Consequences:** Screens that already break M-4 surface on their scripts' next run; each is fixed or marked `data-margins-ok` with its reason in the PR that adds the check. A visual PR without its "measured" line is not ready to merge.
