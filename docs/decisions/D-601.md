## D-601 · Tembleque launches as the owner's two prototypes, on the home page only
**Date:** 2026-11-21 · **Status:** current · **Relation:** follows D-517 (a game launched by flag), here for a game of its own folder like ¡Al toque!; leaves out La Copa and Uno al día from LAUNCH.md's checklist for this game · **Topics:** structure, home
**Context:** ⚖️ Tembleque (`wobbles`) came into the lab as two finished pages the owner built with another tool, kept whole as delivered (easy in 2D, hard in 3D, behind one cover). The owner asked (2026-11-21) to prepare its launch, and chose its scope: the home page only. La Copa and Uno al día need a version built on the house's engine (one screen in `cup/games/`, a 0–100 score, a seed), which the prototypes are not.
**Decision:**
- **Out of the lab as it is:** the cover and both levels move from `/labs/wobbles/` to `/wobbles/` (the old paths, levels included, are bridges, C-18), with a `games.js` entry from its `game.json` behind `lanza: 'wobbles'`. Once the flag is *all* it is a game like any other on the home page, in the search and the filters, and in Rankings, one tab per level (`wobbles_easy`, `wobbles_hard`, `SOLO_LEVELS`).
- **Not in La Copa or Uno al día**, no `copa` and no `unoAlDia`, until a house-engine version exists; that is later work.
- **Its canon exceptions stay** (C-2: no `engine.js` or `engine.test.mjs`, the physics lives in each level's page; C-6: no saved game; C-7: no `compartir.js`, no `trackStart`/`trackFinish`), now also in `games.test.mjs`'s `EXCEPCIONES` and `docs/memory-debt.json`, since it is no longer a lab game.
**Why:** the owner wants the game in players' hands now; rewriting it on the house engine would change the game the owner tuned, and the levels already record their scores and show the shared result (C-19).
**Consequences:** the cleanup PR after the launch removes `lanza` and the `FLAGS` row as for any launch. A later PR that rebuilds it on the house engine drops these exceptions and brings it to La Copa and Uno al día.
