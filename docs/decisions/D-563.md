## D-563 · Uno al día's month table is the same on every phone: every reader adds every player's uncounted days
**Date:** 2026-11-16 · **Status:** current · **Relation:** amends D-500 (who sees which pending points) · **Topics:** random-and-daily, rankings
**Context:** With the night's totals (D-500) only the hourly rebuild writes the month's points, and /today/ added the pending days to the viewer's own row only. Two players then saw each other in different places: Mica saw herself at 2014 (+131 today) and Chan at 1781; Chan saw himself at 1870 (+89) and Mica at 1883. The ranking under a finished game had no correction at all, so the player's own row lacked today's points and streak bonus until they came back to /today/.
**Decision:**
- **One projection, inside the read** (`proyectarMes` in `records.js`, called by `J.tabla` and `J.tablaAmigos` in `jugador.js` for `uno-al-dia-pts`): to the rebuild's rows it adds, for every player, the days in the day tables (`records/uno-al-dia/d<n>`, yesterday, today and tomorrow by the phone's clock) after the row's last counted day `u`: the score plus the streak bonus, the streak going on from the row's `r` when the day follows `u`, else from 1. A player with no month row yet gets one. Every screen that reads the month (/today/, the ranking under the result, /records/, the home card's chip) gets it, so they all agree, on every phone.
- The rebuild stays the only writer (D-500); `conMiCuenta` still adds what only this phone knows (a day not uploaded yet).
**Why:** the owner asked for absolute consistency: two players must never see different numbers for the same person.
**Consequences:** up to three more small reads per month-table read (the day tables, at most 100 rows each). A day a 🧊 saved, or the first day of a month for a player on a streak, shows a smaller bonus until the hourly rebuild counts it, the same on every phone. `records.test.mjs` covers it with the Mica and Chan case.
