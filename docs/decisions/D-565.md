## D-565 · The heartbeat: GitHub's frequent jobs in one relay, measured, with a watchdog
**Date:** 2026-11-16 · **Status:** current · **Relation:** amends D-564, D-500, D-233 · **Topics:** pwa
**Context:** D-564 turned notifications.yml into a relay because GitHub's 15-minute cron ran every 4–10 hours. The same happened to every frequent schedule: Uno al día's hourly rebuild (D-500) ran 14 times in four days instead of ~90, and nobody noticed, because only the last round's time was recorded. The daily jobs do run. The owner asked (2026-11-16) for the notification services to be more reliable and easier to measure, all on GitHub.
**Decision:**
- **One heartbeat:** notifications.yml's relay runs Uno al día's rebuild every hour too (`tools/jobs/uad-hourly.sh`); uad-rebuild.yml loses its cron and stays to run it by hand. A new frequent job goes into the heartbeat, not into its own cron.
- **Measured:** every round writes `stats/prod/push/dias/<YYYY-MM-DD Pacific>` = `{ vueltas, maxHueco, uad }` (`tools/push/heartbeat.mjs`): the rounds, the longest gap in minutes (the most a notification waited for a round) and the rebuilds; `uadAt` the last rebuild. 60 days are kept.
- **Seen:** the panel's 🔔 block shows the longest gap and the rounds in the range; a round older than 20 minutes is marked.
- **Watched:** round-watch.yml (daily, which GitHub does run) opens the "Heartbeat behind" issue when there was no round for 30 minutes, no rebuild for 3 hours, or yesterday had a gap over 15 minutes or fewer than 20 rebuilds; it closes it on its own (`tools/push/heartbeat-watch.mjs`).
**Why:** GitHub's cron cannot be trusted for anything more frequent than daily, and a job that fails quietly is found only by a player. One long run is one thing to watch, and a number per day says whether it worked.
**Consequences:** The relay depends on GitHub queueing the next run within ~5h40m; when it does not, the gap shows in the panel and the next day's issue. The Mac mini and Cloudflare stay out of it (the owner, D-564).
