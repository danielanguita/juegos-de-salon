## D-614 · Offline: a still plate on the home page, the palette alone everywhere else (in Labs)
**Date:** 2026-11-23 · **Status:** current · **Relation:** amends D-609 (its sticker on covers and setup screens) · **Topics:** pwa
**Context:** The offline sticker «📴 Sin conexión» was placed from the home page's dancing emoji, so it jumped around and sometimes touched the title; on the games' covers it was one more label on top of a palette that already says it. Pending uploads were folded into the same sticker. The owner compared five treatments drawn as Candlelight phone mockups and chose "Placa" for the home page (2026-11-23).
**Decision:**
- **Home page**: while offline, the hero's dancing emoji gives its place to a still plate, 📴 over a pill «SIN CONEXIÓN» (es/en/pt), in the same box: nothing moves on entering or leaving the mode, it never covers the title, and a tap opens the offline sheet.
- **Every other view** (covers, setup, Uno al día, /labs/, the profile, Rankings, play screens): **no label**; Candlelight alone says the app is offline.
- **Pending uploads apart**: on the home page a line at the bottom says «⏳ 2 partidas esperan señal para subir» for a few seconds; it leaves when they upload.
- **Behind the lab**: flag `extended-offline` (`offline-ui.js`); with it off the sticker is as D-609 left it. The owner publishes it from its `/labs/` card; a later PR removes the gate and the old sticker.
**Why:** the colour change is enough to tell a player they are offline; one fixed sign where the app starts is clearer than a moving sticker on every screen.
**Consequences:** `tools/e2e/home/extended-offline.mjs` measures the plate at 390 and 320 px (the title does not move, nothing covered). Unchanged: the dimmed «📡 Solo con señal» links, the sheet on first entering the mode, D-609's immediate switch.
