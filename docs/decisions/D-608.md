## D-608 · La Copa: a new cup's audience starts on the phone's zone, not its language
**Date:** 2026-11-23 · **Status:** current · **Relation:** amends D-187 (the audience starts on the creator's language's) and D-521 (the zone follows the audience); behind the `cup-zone-phone` flag (D-498) · **Topics:** cup
**Context:** A group living only in the United States created a cup in Spanish. Spanish started the audience on 🇨🇱 Chile (D-187), and Chile's audience brings Chile's time (D-521), so every day opened at 19:00 and closed at 18:59 for all of them. The create screen says «hora de Chile · Cambiar», but nobody noticed it. The owner asked for the fix on 2026-11-23.
**Decision:** On the new create screen, a new cup's audience starts on the country of the phone's zone: a phone in Chile on 🇨🇱 Chile, one in Brazil on 🇧🇷 Brazil, any other on 🌎 Global, whose zone is the phone's (`publicoDelHuso` in `engine.js`). The admin can still pick Chile or Brazil, which brings that country's time. A cup created from another one («Otra copa con este grupo») keeps that cup's audience. Without the flag, the language decides as before.
**Why:** The language says what a player speaks, not where the group lives; the phone's zone does. A Chilean abroad who wants Chile's content picks it in one tap, and sees which zone that brings.
**Consequences:** In Spanish outside Chile, a new cup's content is Global (D-187: neither Chile's nor Brazil's local cards). The cups already created keep their `meta.tz` and `meta.aud`. Published by flipping `flags/cup-zone-phone` to all; a later PR removes the gate and the old default.
