- **Profile (Labs): tap any name in any ranking to see that player's profile.** It works on every table with names: a game's cover and result, Rankings, Uno al día, La Copa's champions, and a Cup's own table (for players signed in with their name). The sheet opens with where you tapped it: that table and tab, their place and score as the row showed them, and their all-time place in that table when you came from the week's or friends' tab. From a Cup's table it says the Cup, their place and points.
